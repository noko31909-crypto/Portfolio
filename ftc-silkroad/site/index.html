    How it works

    1. Post in the Telegram bot

      The bot asks what you have, what you want for it, and for a photo. Your team name, city and Telegram username come from your team’s registration.

    2. Teams find it here

      Every active listing shows on this site. Anyone can search by part, city or team without an account.

    3. Agree in a private chat

      Tap the username on a listing to open a Telegram chat with that team. You agree on the price and the hand-over between yourselves.

    FTC Silkroad doesn’t handle payments or delivery. Teams agree on both in Telegram.

    Pictures marked “Catalog photo” load from the part maker’s website and belong to that maker. They show the product, not the team’s actual part.
